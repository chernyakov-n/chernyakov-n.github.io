Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

In short: I collect only what I need to reply to your message and to see which work gets watched. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. This policy follows the Law of the Republic of Belarus No. 99-Z of 7 May 2021 “On Personal Data Protection”.

Who processes the data

The data controller is Nikita Chernyakov, self-employed, Minsk, Belarus. For any question about your data, write to hello@chernyakov.pro.

What data

  • From the contact form — your name, a way to reach you (email, phone or Telegram) and the message. Only what you write yourself.
  • Anonymous statistics — which pages and videos were opened, country (derived from the IP address at the moment of the request; the IP address itself is not stored), device type (phone, tablet or computer), page language and the website you came from. This data cannot tell who you are.
  • Cookies — “lang” remembers the site language you chose for a year; a technical sign-in cookie is used only by the site owner in the admin area. No advertising or third-party cookies.

Why

To reply to your message and discuss your project. Statistics help me understand which work people watch and improve the site.

Legal basis

Your consent. You give it by ticking the box before sending the form. Without consent the form will not be sent — but you can always write by email or messenger instead.

How long it is kept

Messages — up to 3 years, then deleted. Statistics — up to 25 months. The language cookie — 1 year.

Transfer abroad

Messages from the form are delivered to my mailbox at Google (Gmail, USA), and a notification about a new message goes to Telegram. This is a cross-border transfer: these countries may not ensure an adequate level of protection of data subjects’ rights. It happens only with your consent. The data is not shared with anyone else.

Your rights

At any time you can withdraw your consent, ask what data I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Just write to hello@chernyakov.pro.

I will reply within the time limits set by law. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can complain to the National Personal Data Protection Center of the Republic of Belarus.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
